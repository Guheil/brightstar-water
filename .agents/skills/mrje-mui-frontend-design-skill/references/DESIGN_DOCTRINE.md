# Design Doctrine

## Desired character

Professional, modern, trustworthy, warm, operational, local-business appropriate.

## Avoid

- gradients
- purple gradients
- glassmorphism
- neon
- glowing blobs
- bento-grid dependency
- excessive cards
- giant hero typography
- tiny eyebrow labels
- status-pill overload
- fake analytics
- generic AI copy
- animation everywhere

## Customer

Editorial and image-led where useful.

## Admin

Dense and task-oriented.

## Deliverer

Mobile-first and direct.
